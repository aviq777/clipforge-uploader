Clipforge Uploader

Clipforge Uploader is a desktop tool for editing short gaming clips: it trims pauses, frames the action for vertical video and adds readable captions.

When a creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, the app can send a finished clip to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft through the Content Posting API. The creator then reviews, edits and posts the draft in the TikTok app. The app never posts on its own.
